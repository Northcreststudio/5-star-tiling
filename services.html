Services

Residential and commercial tile installation

Specializing in both residential and commercial tile installation, 5 Star Tiling provides quality workmanship and high-end finishes in our Calgary projects — each with their unique challenges and opportunities to enhance style.

01 · Kitchen Tile

Kitchens, the heart of the home

Whether updating an existing design or looking to choose the perfect tile and countertop combo for your new kitchen, speak with us. We will flawlessly execute on glass, ceramic, and porcelain backsplash or kitchen island tile installations, bringing your designs to life.
