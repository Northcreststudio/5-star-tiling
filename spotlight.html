Project spotlight · Hillhurst, Northwest Calgary

Natural, elegant design details give a timeless feel to these custom bathrooms

These custom residential tiling projects in Northwest Calgary’s Hillhurst community feature natural, hand-made stone tile in dark tones to provide a luxurious feel and accent the white bath and vanity fixtures.

Natural lines of the tile and light grout in the shower enclosure and along the floors exude a stylishly classic look while providing contrast to the tub and shower surrounds.

Mosaic tile accents line the wall of the custom shower enclosure and are mirrored in the tiled drain and ceiling of the shower.

  • Shower system
  • Bath Surround
  • Mosaic Feature Tile
  • Flooring

Dark grout in a similar tone to a dark tile will create a softer look,

while a light grout will bring out the tile pattern, adding even more drama.
