About 5 Star Tiling

Award-winning service from your local Calgary Master Tilers

5 Star Tiling is a full service tile installation and home renovation contractor serving the city of Calgary and surrounding area including Cochrane, Okotoks, Airdrie and Chestermere.

In his 12 years of experience in the industry, the company’s founder, Frederik Pashkja, has worked with hundreds of Calgary home builders and home owners and is known for his dedication to craftsmanship and professional service.

5 Star Tiling has won multiple Best of the Best and Best of awards from HomeStars.com (with a 5-star rating and near perfect star score from 50+ reviews). Named one of the best tile contractors in Canada by Canada Best, and with 75+ 5-star Google Reviews under our belt, our high industry standard has been continuously recognized across the years.

The 5 Star Tiling Process

Initial Assessment

  1. 01An Initial Consultation where we meet with you and assess the scope of the project
  2. 02With your input we draft a Preconstruction Design that aligns with and delivers on your vision
  3. 03A detailed Project Estimate is provided that will help you budget and avoid overpaying

Construction

  1. 04Continuous Onsite Consultations with regards to the project’s progress are had with you
  2. 05We conduct a thorough Quality Control assessment and add the finishing touches to the installation
  3. 06We carry out a Walk-through of the completed project with you, ensuring that every detail is perfect

Having trouble with your tiles?

We are available to repair worn, damaged, or poorly installed tiles, install new residential tiles, or install tiles for your home renovation project.

We can also answer questions about regrouting, resurfacing, and tile replacement.
